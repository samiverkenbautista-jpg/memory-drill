## 📖 Overview

**EchoCube** is a cognitive training companion for puzzle solvers who want to sharpen their ability to memorize and reconstruct complex spatial states without looking. Where traditional trainers drill algorithms or speed, EchoCube drills *imagination*. It is a sanctuary for the mind's eye — a place where letters, images, and spatial anchors become the scaffolding of an invisible cube you rebuild in memory alone.

Inspired by the growing community of blindfolded solvers, EchoCube reimagines the humble memo trainer as a full sensory gymnasium. Instead of overwhelming you with raw scramble strings, it hands you a curated sequence of memory hooks, visual mnemonics, and progressive difficulty ladders that adapt to how your brain actually stores information.

Whether you are chasing your first successful solve behind closed eyes in 2026 or refining a sub-minute recall pipeline for competition season, EchoCube grows with you.

## ✨ Why EchoCube Exists

Blindfolded cubing is not a party trick. It is a discipline of attention, translation, and trust. Most trainers assume you already know your lettering scheme, your buffers, your parity fixes. They hand you a scramble and a stopwatch and wish you luck.

EchoCube takes a different path. It walks beside you. It whispers the first letter pair. It nudges you when recall latency spikes. It celebrates the quiet moments — the pause before the first move, the breath before execution — because that is where mastery is truly built.

## 🧠 How It Works

EchoCube treats memorization as a pipeline with five distinct stations: **ingest**, **encode**, **consolidate**, **retrieve**, and **execute**. Each station has its own mini-game, its own metrics, and its own philosophy.

1. **Ingest** — The app presents a scramble. You decide how long you want to stare at it.
2. **Encode** — You translate stickers into letters, letters into pictures, pictures into a story.
3. **Consolidate** — A short retention timer counts down while you rehearse mentally.
4. **Retrieve** — The screen hides the scramble. You replay the story in your mind.
5. **Execute** — Optional: solve on a physical cube, then log your result and let the app adjust.

This loop is the heartbeat of EchoCube. It never rushes you. It never shames you. It simply reflects what you did and asks, gently, whether you want to try one more round.

## 🔧 Training Modes

- **Sprint Memo** — Short scrambles, snap decisions, rapid fire.
- **Deep Dive** — Long scrambles with extended consolidation windows.
- **Edge-Only / Corner-Only** — Isolate one lettering system at a time.
- **Pair Roulette** — Random pairs flash on screen for instant recall drills.
- **Parity Pulse** — Timed drills on recognizing and resolving parity states.
- **Endurance Session** — Thirty scrambles in a row. The mental marathon.
- **Ghost Mode** — Solve on a physical cube while the app watches the clock.

## 📊 Analytics That Actually Help

Charts are nice. Actionable charts are better. EchoCube's analytics dashboard surfaces:

- **Recall heatmaps** showing which letter pairs you hesitate on.
- **Latency trends** across days, weeks, and sessions.
- **Consistency scores** measuring how steady your memorization speed is.
- **Accuracy vs. speed curves** to show where you sacrifice one for the other.
- **Streak calendars** honoring the days you showed up.

All data stays on your device unless you explicitly enable sync.

## ⚙️ Non-Traditional Setup Path

EchoCube intentionally avoids command-line ceremony. To begin:

1. Open the hosted web build in any modern browser.
2. Or, for desktop users, launch the packaged app for your platform of choice.
3. Walk through the two-minute onboarding wizard.
4. Pick your lettering scheme, or accept the default Speffz layout.
5. Start your first Sprint Memo round.

No terminals. No dependency trees. No package managers. Just you, a cube, and a quiet room.
